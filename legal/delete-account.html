删除 ColivEase 账号

适用于 ColivEase 网站、Android 和 iOS App（运营方：ColivEase，安大略省）

1. 在 App 或网站里删除（立即生效）

  1. 登录 ColivEase。
  2. 打开「我」页面，往下找到「账号」。
  3. 点「删除账号」，再点「永久删除」确认。

2. 无法登录时，用邮件申请

用你注册 ColivEase 的邮箱发邮件到 support@colivease.ca，标题写「删除账号」。我们核实邮箱后会在 30 天内删除，并回信告诉你。

3. 会删除什么

  • 立即永久删除：资料、头像、照片、房源、匹配偏好、队伍成员身份、申请和邀请、推送设备。
  • 聊天：你发出的消息会留在对方那边，显示为「已注销用户」。
  • 网站上购买的订阅会自动取消。在 Google Play 或 App Store 订阅的，请先在商店的「订阅」里取消，否则商店会继续扣费。

4. 会保留什么、保留多久

  • 购买记录（不含卡号）：按加拿大税法要求保存（一般为 6 年）。
  • 举报和员工审计日志：保存到处理完毕和法律要求的期限为止。
  • 在线签署的合同：保存到租约结束后 1 年，因为另一方也需要这份证据。

Delete your ColivEase account

For the ColivEase website and the Android and iOS apps (operated by ColivEase, Ontario)

1. In the app or on the website (immediate)

  1. Sign in to ColivEase.
  2. Open the "Me" tab and scroll to "Account".
  3. Tap "Delete account", then "Delete permanently".

2. Can't sign in? Ask by email

Write from the email you signed up with to support@colivease.ca with the subject "Delete my account". Once we've confirmed the email is yours we delete the account within 30 days and write back.

3. What is deleted

  • Deleted at once and for good: your profile, avatar, photos, homes, matching preferences, team memberships, applications and invites, and push devices.
  • Chats: messages you sent stay with the other person, shown as from "Deleted user".
  • Subscriptions bought on the website are cancelled automatically. If you subscribed through Google Play or the App Store, cancel it in the store's Subscriptions first, or the store keeps charging.

4. What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchase records (never card numbers): as Canadian tax law requires (generally 6 years).
  • Reports and staff audit logs: until they are handled and as long as the law requires.
  • Contracts signed online: until one year after the lease ends, because the other party needs them as evidence.
