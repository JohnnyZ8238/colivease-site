Location privacy

Your address is never published

Hosts enter their address only to place the home. It instantly becomes a ~350 m area, and the centre of that area isn't the home.

  • The exact address is never shown publicly
  • Only a renter whose application you accept sees it
Scam protection

We spot the scam before it's sprung

"Send the deposit first", "I'm overseas": classic lines trigger an instant warning. Every listing is reviewed by a person before it goes live, agents are licence-checked, and anyone can add an ID-verified badge.

  • AI lease check against Ontario's tenancy rules
  • Legal deposits and authorised sublets, at a glance
