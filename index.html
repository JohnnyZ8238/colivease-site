    Questions

    Something else? Write to support@colivease.ca

    Does ColivEase handle rent or deposits?

    No. ColivEase never touches money between tenants and hosts. Rent and deposits are arranged between you and the host. Never pay anything before a viewing.

    How is the match score worked out?

    It compares the areas you want and your commute, budget, move-in date, whether you study or work, and the languages you share. You can make gender preference a must-have. It follows fixed rules, no AI.

    Who sees my real photos?

    People see your comic avatar by default. You choose, per person and per photo, who sees a real photo, and you can take it back anytime.

    When do I see a home’s exact address?

    When the host accepts your application. Until then, the map shows an area of about 350 m.

    Which areas do you cover?

    The Greater Toronto Area only.

    Is there a phone app?

    The iOS and Android apps are on the way. Until then, ColivEase works in the browser on any phone or computer.

    Do I need an account to look around?

    No. You can browse homes and their safety info without one. To chat with people you need an account, and you must be 18 or older.
