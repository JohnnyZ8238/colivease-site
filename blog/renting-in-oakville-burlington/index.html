Oakville Rentals: A Guide to Oakville and Burlington

Oakville rentals and Burlington rentals compared: main areas, rooms near Sheridan College, the GO train commute to Toronto and what to check before you sign.

Updated November 21, 2026 · 7 min read · ColivEase

Oakville rentals and Burlington rentals give you lakeside towns, quieter streets and a direct GO train line to downtown Toronto, at the cost of a more car-oriented daily life away from the stations. The smartest way to choose is to pick your GO station or campus first, then look for a room, basement or condo within an easy walk or a single bus ride of it.

This guide covers how transit actually works, which areas suit which renters, what students near Sheridan College should know, and what to check before you sign. If you're still comparing the whole GTA, start with our guide to the best neighbourhoods to rent in Toronto.

Key takeaways

  • The GO Lakeshore West line is the backbone. Oakville has Oakville GO and Bronte GO; Burlington has Appleby, Burlington and Aldershot.
  • Local buses connect to GO for free with PRESTO. Both Oakville Transit and Burlington Transit offer free co-fare connections with GO.
  • Sheridan's Trafalgar Road campus is Oakville's main student hub, served by Oakville Transit from Oakville GO.
  • Expect houses, basements and condos. Rooms in detached homes and basement units are common; condos cluster near downtown cores and GO stations.
  • Check basement legality and whether the host lives there, since both change your protections.

Who Oakville and Burlington suit

These towns tend to work well for people who value space and quiet over nightlife: hybrid workers who go downtown a few days a week, Sheridan students, couples, and newcomers with family or a job in Halton. If you want to step out your door into late-night restaurants and the subway every day, a Toronto neighbourhood is likely a better fit. Be honest about how often you'll actually travel into the city.

Getting around: Oakville to Toronto commute by GO train

Metrolinx describes the Lakeshore West line as two-way, all-day service seven days a week between Union Station and Aldershot GO, with some trains continuing into Hamilton. From Oakville GO, the ride to Union is typically well under an hour; trains from Burlington's stations take longer. Check GO Transit's trip planner for the exact train you'd catch, because travel times vary by trip.

Service patterns change. In April 2026, Metrolinx announced the return of 15-minute weekend service between Oakville GO and Union in the afternoons and evenings. Don't assume the same frequency at every station or every hour.

Local buses

  • Oakville Transit feeds Oakville GO and Bronte GO and serves Sheridan's Trafalgar Road campus. Its fare page lists the GO co-fare as free when you tap PRESTO.
  • Burlington Transit runs through its Downtown Terminal on John Street and connects at Burlington, Appleby and Aldershot GO. Burlington says connecting rides with GO are free with PRESTO.
  • Between the two towns, Oakville Transit and Burlington Transit meet at Appleby GO.

Fare rules have fine print (GO e-tickets, for example, don't qualify for some discounts), so read the agency pages before you count on a free transfer.

Driving

Many renters here drive, using the QEW, Highway 403 and Highway 407. If you need parking, ask whether a spot is included or costs extra, and whether street parking is allowed overnight in winter.

Renting near Sheridan College Oakville

Sheridan's Trafalgar Road campus is in Oakville. Sheridan also has campuses in Brampton and Mississauga, so confirm where your program runs before you sign anything.

  • Where students rent: rooms and basements in the surrounding neighbourhoods, especially within a short Oakville Transit ride. Oakville Transit routes link the campus with Oakville GO.
  • On or off campus: Sheridan has an on-campus residence at Trafalgar, but many students rent rooms off campus, often in shared houses.
  • Timing: demand rises before each intake, so start looking several weeks before your term.
  • Live-in hosts: many student rooms are in homes where the owner lives. If you share their kitchen or bathroom, the Residential Tenancies Act usually doesn't apply, so get the notice period and deposit terms in writing.

Burlington's location between Oakville and Hamilton suits households where one person works toward Toronto and the other toward Hamilton.

Oakville rentals and Burlington rentals: what you'll find

  • Rooms in houses: the most affordable option, often with a live-in owner or a group of roommates.
  • Basement apartments: common across both towns. Our guide to legal basement apartments explains what to look for.
  • Condos: concentrated in the downtown cores and around GO stations.
  • Purpose-built rental towers and townhouses: scattered through older and newer areas alike.

What to check before you sign

  • Commute test: do the trip at rush hour, door to platform.
  • Basement basics: a proper exit, smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, and whether it's a legal unit.
  • Who lives there: sharing a kitchen or bathroom with the owner usually means the RTA doesn't apply.
  • Rent rules: many units first occupied after November 15, 2018 aren't covered by the annual rent increase guideline. Ask when the unit was first rented.
  • Utilities and parking: what's included, and how costs are split between roommates.
  • The lease: most tenancies need the Ontario Standard Lease. Never pay before you've seen the unit and signed.

Frequently asked questions

Is Oakville or Burlington better for commuting to Toronto?

Both sit on the GO Lakeshore West line, so the deciding factor is usually how close you live to a station. Oakville is closer to Union, while Burlington adds more train time but offers three stations to choose from. Check GO's trip planner from the exact address before you commit.

Can I live in Oakville without a car?

It's possible if you choose carefully. Living within walking distance of a GO station, downtown Oakville or a frequent Oakville Transit route makes it workable. Many suburban streets, especially in the north, are designed around driving, so test your daily trips first.

Is Burlington cheaper than Oakville for renters?

It depends on the unit type and area, and prices change month to month, so avoid relying on general claims. Compare current listings for the same kind of place, such as a room in a house or a one-bedroom condo, and include transit or parking costs in the total.

Are basement apartments in Oakville and Burlington legal?

Many are, but not all. Ask the landlord whether the unit is a legal secondary or additional residential unit and look for basics like a proper exit, smoke and carbon monoxide alarms and separate heating controls. If you're unsure, contact the Town of Oakville or City of Burlington.

Should I rent near Oakville GO or near Sheridan?

Choose based on where you go most days. If you commute to Toronto, walking distance to a GO station saves the most time; if you study at Sheridan, a short bus ride to campus matters more. Students with jobs or classes in both places often look along the Trafalgar Road corridor, which buses link to both campus and Oakville GO.
