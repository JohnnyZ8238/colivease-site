Rent in Markham: 2026 Guide to Areas, Transit and Rooms

Thinking of where to rent in Markham? Compare Unionville, Downtown Markham and Thornhill, find rooms, plan the commute downtown and check basement registration.

Updated November 21, 2026 · 7 min read · ColivEase

To rent in Markham, most people choose between condos along Highway 7 in Downtown Markham, rooms and basement suites in houses across Unionville, Milliken and Cornell, and townhouses in newer subdivisions. Markham suits renters who want quieter streets, excellent food and, for many newcomers, a large Chinese-speaking community, while staying within reach of Toronto by GO train or YRT and Viva buses. Your exact address decides how easy daily life is, so plan around transit before price.

Key takeaways

  • Condos cluster along Highway 7 in Downtown Markham and near Woodbine and Leslie; rooms and basements are spread across the suburbs.
  • Transit runs east to west on Highway 7 (Viva) and north to south on the Stouffville GO line, with YRT buses linking to TTC Line 1 at Finch.
  • One Fare makes YRT-to-TTC transfers free with PRESTO, debit or credit.
  • York University's Markham Campus opened in 2024 on Highway 7 near Unionville GO.
  • Basement apartments must be registered with the City of Markham, so ask the landlord for proof.
  • Markham has a large Chinese community, with Cantonese- and Mandarin-speaking landlords and roommates easy to find.

Rent in Markham: what kinds of homes are available

Markham's rental stock looks different from downtown Toronto's:

  • Condos in Downtown Markham (around Highway 7 and Warden or Kennedy), along Highway 7 near Woodbine and Leslie, and in the Thornhill area. Usually owned by individual investors.
  • Basement apartments in detached and semi-detached homes, common in Milliken, Unionville, Cornell and Berczy Village.
  • Rooms in houses, often with a shared kitchen and sometimes with the owner living upstairs.
  • Townhouses shared by roommate groups, especially in newer areas like Cornell and Cathedraltown.

On pricing, Rentals.ca's Markham page showed an average one-bedroom asking rent of about $2,005 in March 2026. Rooms in shared houses typically cost less than a one-bedroom, but price depends heavily on whether utilities, internet and parking are included. Rooms for rent in Markham

Most rooms for rent in Markham are in single-family homes. Some are basement bedrooms, some are upstairs rooms with a shared kitchen, and some are full basement suites split between two roommates. Here's what to check:

  • Who lives there? If you share a kitchen or bathroom with the owner, the Residential Tenancies Act usually doesn't apply, which changes your notice and eviction protections.
  • What's included? Utilities, internet and laundry are often bundled into the room price, but ask.
  • Is the suite registered? The City of Markham requires all two-unit houses to be registered, and both units are inspected for building and fire safety. When the process is complete, Markham Fire and Emergency Services issues a letter confirming registration, so ask to see it. Even if a unit isn't registered, your tenant rights generally still apply; our legal basement apartment guide explains what to look for.
  • How far is the bus? In newer subdivisions, the nearest frequent route may be a long walk away.

Chinese community in Markham

Markham is home to one of Canada's largest Chinese-Canadian communities. Plazas such as Pacific Mall near Kennedy and Steeles and First Markham Place on Highway 7 are packed with restaurants, bakeries and services, and many listings are posted in Chinese on WeChat groups, Xiaohongshu (RED), 51.ca and YorkBBS. These channels can be useful, but rental scams circulate there too. Verify the landlord, view the place in person or on a live video call, and don't send a deposit until you've reviewed a written lease.

Markham to downtown commute

There are three main ways to get from Markham to downtown Toronto:

  1. Stouffville GO line. Stations include Milliken, Unionville, Centennial, Markham and Mount Joy, with trains to Union Station. Service is busiest at rush hour, so check midday, evening and weekend trains for your schedule.
  2. YRT or Viva to the TTC. Buses run to Finch station on Line 1, then you take the subway south. Under One Fare, the transfer from YRT to the TTC is free when you pay with PRESTO, debit or credit.
  3. Driving. Highway 404 and the Don Valley Parkway connect Markham to downtown, but rush-hour traffic and downtown parking costs add up.

Commute times vary widely by address and time of day, so use a trip planner for your exact start and end points at the hour you'll travel. Living within walking distance of a GO station or the Highway 7 Viva corridor usually makes the biggest difference.

Campuses in and near Markham

York University's Markham Campus opened in 2024 in Downtown Markham, close to Unionville GO station and the Highway 7 Viva corridor. Seneca's former Markham campus at Highway 7 and Highway 404 was temporarily closed at the end of 2024, with programs moved to other locations, so confirm where your classes actually run before signing a lease. Seneca's Newnham campus is a short trip south-west, at Finch Avenue East and Highway 404 in North York.

Which Markham area suits you?

  • Students at York University's Markham Campus: Downtown Markham or Unionville keeps you within walking distance or a short Viva ride of class, and close to Unionville GO for trips into Toronto.
  • Downtown Toronto commuters: Pick a home within walking distance of a Stouffville line station, or in the Thornhill area with a quick bus to Finch station.
  • Tech and office workers in Markham: Condos along Highway 7 between Woodbine and Leslie put you close to the office parks near Highway 404.
  • Roommate groups on a budget: A shared townhouse in Cornell or Cathedraltown can cost less per person than separate rooms, as long as everyone is comfortable with longer bus trips.

Tips before you sign

  • Confirm the landlord's identity and, for a condo, that they own the unit or are a registered agent.
  • Make sure you get the Ontario standard lease for most rentals, not a homemade agreement.
  • Don't pay more than a last month's rent deposit as a condition of renting.
  • Get every promise, such as parking or furniture, in writing.

This is general information, not legal advice. For your situation, contact the Landlord and Tenant Board or a community legal clinic.

Frequently asked questions

Is Markham a good place to live for young professionals?

It can be, especially if you work in Markham's tech and office parks along Highway 7 and Highway 404, or downtown with a home near a GO station. You get newer condos, great food and quieter streets than central Toronto. The trade-off is fewer late-night options and more reliance on buses or a car.

Do I need to speak Chinese to rent in Markham?

No. English works everywhere, and leases in Ontario follow the same rules whatever language you negotiate in. That said, many landlords and roommates in Markham speak Cantonese or Mandarin, which can be a real comfort for newcomers.

Where do York University Markham Campus students live?

Many look at condos and rooms in Downtown Markham and Unionville, within walking distance or a short bus ride of the campus on Highway 7. Others live along Viva and YRT routes or near Stouffville line GO stations. Compare commute times before choosing a cheaper room further away.

Can I live in Markham without a car?

Yes, if you live near Highway 7 Viva service, a frequent YRT route or a Stouffville line GO station. Many suburban streets have infrequent buses, so check evening and weekend schedules for your exact address before signing.
