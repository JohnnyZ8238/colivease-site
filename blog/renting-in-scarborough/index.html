Scarborough Rooms for Rent: 2026 Renter's Guide

Looking at Scarborough rooms for rent? Compare areas near UTSC and Centennial College, how transit works since Line 3 closed, and what to check before you sign.

Updated November 21, 2026 · 7 min read · ColivEase

Scarborough rooms for rent are usually easiest to find in three places: around UTSC in Highland Creek and West Hill, around Scarborough Centre and Centennial's Progress campus, and in established neighbourhoods like Agincourt near GO and bus links. Rooms here tend to cost less than comparable rooms downtown, and you often get more space. The trade-off is transit: since Line 3 closed, almost every trip starts with a bus, so pick your street around your commute.

This guide covers how getting around actually works in 2026, which areas suit which renters, and the checks that matter most for the kinds of homes you will see listed in Scarborough.

Key takeaways

  • Commute first, room second. Line 2 ends at Kennedy; most of Scarborough is reached by bus, the new Scarborough Busway, Line 5 or GO.
  • Students have two hubs. UTSC in the east (Highland Creek) and Centennial's Progress campus near Scarborough Centre.
  • Houses dominate. Expect many rooms in detached homes and basement units, plus condos around Scarborough Centre and older rental towers.
  • Licensing matters. A house renting many rooms separately should hold a City multi-tenant house licence.
  • The subway extension is coming, not here. Don't sign based on a future station.

Getting around Scarborough in 2026

Transit in Scarborough changed a lot in the last few years, so older advice online is often out of date.

  • Line 2 Bloor-Danforth runs east to Kennedy station, which is now the end of the line.
  • Line 3 Scarborough closed in 2023 and was replaced by buses. On September 30, 2026, the TTC opened the Scarborough Busway on part of the old Line 3 corridor, giving buses their own road between Kennedy and Scarborough Centre.
  • Line 5 Eglinton opened in February 2026 and meets Line 2 at Kennedy, which makes Kennedy a major transfer point for trips west along Eglinton.
  • The Scarborough Subway Extension (Line 2 east to Sheppard and McCowan) is under construction. Tunnelling is well along, but no firm opening date has been confirmed, so treat it as a long-term plus.
  • GO Transit has stations on the Lakeshore East line (such as Guildwood and Rouge Hill) and the Stouffville line (such as Kennedy, Agincourt and Milliken). Under Ontario's One Fare Program, transfers between GO and the TTC are free within the time window when you pay with PRESTO, debit or credit.

Tip: Before you book a viewing, map the trip from the exact address at the time you would actually travel. In Scarborough, being a five-minute walk from a frequent or express route can matter more than being close to a station on paper. Renting near UTSC

Where do students rent near UTSC?

The University of Toronto Scarborough sits on Military Trail in Highland Creek. The closest options are:

  • Highland Creek and West Hill: quiet, leafy streets of detached houses. Many owners rent rooms or basement units to students, and some houses are shared by several students.
  • Morningside and Morningside Heights: newer subdivisions north of the 401, with rooms in family homes and basements. Check bus frequency, since some streets are less well served.
  • Scarborough Centre: further away but a big bus hub, with condos, the mall and Civic Centre. A good pick if you want city-style living and a direct express or busway connection.

Several TTC routes serve the campus, including express buses from Kennedy and other hubs. Check UTSC's own transit page, because route numbers and patterns change.

Rent near UTSC: what to expect from the homes

Most rooms near campus are in owner-occupied houses. That matters legally: if you share a kitchen or bathroom with the owner or their close family who live there, Ontario's Residential Tenancies Act generally does not apply, so you rely on your written agreement. Ask at the viewing who lives in the house, and whether the owner shares the kitchen.

Centennial College housing

Which Centennial campus is yours?

Centennial College has several campuses. The two in Scarborough most students ask about are:

  • Progress campus on Progress Avenue, near Scarborough Centre and Highway 401. It is the largest campus and has an on-campus residence.
  • Morningside campus, close to UTSC, so the Highland Creek advice above applies.

Where to live for the Progress campus

For Progress, look at Scarborough Centre, the Woburn area along Markham Road and Ellesmere, and nearby streets around Progress Avenue. These keep the bus ride short and give you shops and groceries nearby. Students who want a bigger social circle sometimes live further west along Line 2 and ride the busway, but that adds a transfer.

Scarborough rooms for rent: what kinds of rentals you will find

  • Rooms in houses. The most common listing. Sometimes the owner lives upstairs; sometimes a tenant rents the whole house and sublets rooms.
  • Basement apartments. Plentiful and often good value. Check ceiling height, windows you could exit through, and a working smoke and carbon monoxide alarm. Our guide to legal basement apartments in Toronto walks through the signs.
  • Condos. Clustered around Scarborough Centre, Kennedy and along Sheppard. Expect building rules, fobs and elevator bookings.
  • Purpose-built rental towers. Older apartment buildings along major roads, often with on-site management.
  • Multi-tenant houses. Since March 31, 2024, rooming houses are permitted across Toronto, but operators need a City licence.

What to watch for before you sign in Scarborough

  • Who are you renting from? Owner, tenant subletting rooms, or agent. Ask to see proof the person can rent the room.
  • Is the owner sharing the kitchen or bathroom? If yes, your protections may differ.
  • Is there a written lease? Most tenancies covered by the Act must use Ontario's standard lease.
  • Deposits. In covered tenancies, a landlord can collect a last month's rent deposit and a refundable key deposit, not a "damage deposit."
  • Safety basics. Working smoke and CO alarms, a second way out of basement units, and a lock on your bedroom door if it is a shared house.
  • Too-good prices and pressure. Scams target students searching from overseas. Never pay before you have seen the room, live or in person.

This is general information, not legal advice. If you are unsure whether the Residential Tenancies Act covers your room, contact the Landlord and Tenant Board or a community legal clinic.

Frequently asked questions

Where should I rent near UTSC?

Highland Creek, West Hill and the Morningside area are the closest residential pockets, and many houses there rent rooms or basement units to students. Scarborough Centre is a good second choice if you want condos, a mall and a bus hub. Check the bus route from the exact address before you sign, since walking distance varies a lot.

Is there student housing for Centennial College?

Centennial's Progress campus has an on-campus residence, but many students rent rooms off campus. Popular choices are the neighbourhoods around Scarborough Centre, Progress Avenue and Markham Road, which keep the bus ride short. Centennial also has a Morningside campus close to UTSC, so confirm which campus your program uses.

Is Scarborough a good place to rent without a car?

It can be, if you choose carefully. Line 2 ends at Kennedy, the Scarborough Busway links Kennedy with Scarborough Centre, Line 5 Eglinton meets Line 2 at Kennedy, and GO trains serve stations on the Lakeshore East and Stouffville lines. Living near one of those hubs or a frequent bus route makes car-free life much easier.

When will the Scarborough Subway Extension open?

It is under construction, with tunnelling well advanced, but there is no firm opening date you can plan a lease around. Choose a home that works with today's buses and trains, and treat the subway as a future bonus.

Are rooming houses legal in Scarborough?

Since March 31, 2024, multi-tenant (rooming) houses are permitted across all of Toronto, including Scarborough, but operators need a City licence. If a house rents many rooms to separate people, ask whether it is licensed and check basic fire safety such as smoke and carbon monoxide alarms.
