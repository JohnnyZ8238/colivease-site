North York Apartments for Rent: 2026 Area Guide

Searching North York apartments for rent? Compare Yonge and Finch, North York Centre, Bayview Village and York University areas, plus TTC tips and rules.

Updated November 21, 2026 · 7 min read · ColivEase

North York apartments for rent are mostly high-rise condos along Yonge Street between Sheppard and Steeles, older rental towers, and rooms and basement suites in houses. North York sits inside the City of Toronto with direct subway service on Line 1, so it suits students at York University and Seneca, newcomers who want a strong Chinese or Korean community nearby, and professionals who want more space without losing a one-seat ride downtown. The best area for you depends on which line and campus you'll use every day.

Key takeaways

  • Condos line Yonge Street from Sheppard to Finch and cluster near Bayview Village and Don Mills; houses with rooms and basements fill the side streets.
  • Line 1 is the backbone, with Line 4 Sheppard running east and Line 6 Finch West, open since December 2025, running west.
  • Finch station is a major hub where TTC, YRT and GO buses meet, which makes it handy for York Region commuters.
  • York University has two subway stations on its Keele campus; Seneca Newnham is reached by bus.
  • City of Toronto rules apply: rooming houses need a licence, and renovictions are covered by the City's rental renovation licence bylaw.
  • Chinese and Korean communities are strong along Yonge Street, with many Mandarin-, Cantonese- and Korean-speaking landlords.

North York apartments for rent: what's available

North York is one of the largest parts of Toronto, so the rental mix varies a lot:

  • Condos along Yonge Street (Sheppard, North York Centre and Finch stations), at Bayview Village and around Don Mills and Sheppard. Most are rented by individual owners.
  • Purpose-built rental towers from past decades, often roomier than new condos and, if first occupied before November 15, 2018, covered by rent control.
  • Rooms and basement apartments in houses throughout Willowdale, Newtonbrook, Don Valley Village and the York University area.
  • Student houses near the Keele campus, where several rooms are rented separately.

Rent near Yonge and Finch

Yonge and Finch is one of the most popular places to rent in North York, especially for newcomers and anyone commuting from York Region. Finch station on Line 1 is a major bus terminal, served by TTC buses plus YRT and GO buses heading north. Condos and rental towers line Yonge Street, and the streets on either side are full of houses with basement suites and rooms.

Daily life is easy here: Korean and Chinese restaurants, bakeries, grocery stores and services stretch along Yonge, and many landlords and roommates speak Mandarin, Cantonese or Korean. A few things to keep in mind:

  • Subway extension construction. The Yonge North Subway Extension is being built north from Finch, so expect work zones around Yonge near Steeles.
  • Rush-hour crowds. Finch station gets busy; if you can, view a place at the time you'd commute.
  • Rooms in houses. Ask whether the owner lives there and whether you'll share a kitchen or bathroom with them, since that can mean the Residential Tenancies Act doesn't apply.

Rooms near York University

York University's Keele campus has two stations on Line 1: York University and Pioneer Village. Line 6 Finch West, which opened on December 7, 2025, links to Line 1 at Finch West station and runs west to Humber College, opening up more options along the Finch West corridor.

Rooms near York University are typically in houses in the neighbourhoods around campus and along Line 1 to the south, including Downsview and the area around Sheppard West. Many houses rent several rooms separately to students. That can be affordable and social, but in Toronto, multi-tenant (rooming) houses have needed a City licence since March 31, 2024, and operators must post the licence at the entrance. If a house rents many rooms separately and the landlord can't show a licence, be cautious.

York's own off-campus housing resources are a good place to start, and our guide to the best neighbourhoods in Toronto for students compares areas near every major campus.

Seneca Newnham students

Seneca's Newnham campus is at 1750 Finch Avenue East, near Highway 404. Students often rent around Don Mills station on Line 4, in Don Valley Village, or near Finch station on Line 1, then take a bus along Finch. Check bus frequency for your class times before you sign.

Which North York area suits you?

  • York University students: Rooms around the Keele campus or along Line 1 south toward Downsview keep the commute short. Check rooming house licences.
  • Seneca Newnham students: Don Mills and Sheppard or Don Valley Village, with a bus along Finch or from Don Mills station.
  • Downtown commuters: Anywhere within walking distance of a Line 1 station on Yonge, from Sheppard-Yonge to Finch, gives you a direct ride south.
  • Newcomers looking for community: Yonge and Finch and the Willowdale side streets offer Chinese and Korean groceries, restaurants and services within walking distance.
  • People who want quiet: Bayview Village and the residential streets east of Yonge feel calmer while staying close to Line 4.

What to watch for when renting in North York

  • Basement units. Toronto's zoning allows secondary suites in most houses, but a unit should also meet the Building Code and Fire Code. Check for a second exit and working smoke and carbon monoxide alarms.
  • Renovictions. Since July 31, 2025, Toronto landlords who serve an N13 to renovate must apply for a City rental renovation licence within seven days. An N13 is not an eviction order; only the Landlord and Tenant Board can order you out.
  • Older apartment towers. Rental buildings with three or more storeys and 10 or more units fall under the City's RentSafeTO program. Report repairs to your landlord in writing first; if they go unresolved, you can contact 311.
  • Rent control. Units first occupied after November 15, 2018 have no cap on future increases, which is common in new North York condos. Factor that into your plans.
  • Scams. Scammers target students and newcomers before each term, often with copied condo listings. Our guide to rental scams in Toronto explains the warning signs. Be extra careful with listings found on WeChat groups, Xiaohongshu, 51.ca or YorkBBS.

This is general information, not legal advice. For your situation, contact the Landlord and Tenant Board or a community legal clinic.

Frequently asked questions

Is North York part of Toronto?

Yes. North York was a separate city until it amalgamated into the City of Toronto in 1998. Today it's served by the TTC, and City of Toronto bylaws, including rooming house licensing and the rental renovation licence, apply.

Is it cheaper to rent in North York than downtown?

Often, though not always. Many renters find more space for the money in North York, especially a few stops from the subway, while new condos right at major stations can price close to central Toronto. Compare total costs, including transit and utilities, before deciding.

Where do Seneca Newnham students live?

Seneca's Newnham campus is near Finch Avenue East and Highway 404, so many students rent around Don Mills station on Line 4, in Don Valley Village, or near Finch station on Line 1, then take a bus. Check how often your bus runs at the times your classes start and finish.

How long does it take to get downtown from North York?

It depends on where you start, but most of North York along Yonge Street is on Line 1, with a direct subway ride to downtown. Use the TTC trip planner for your exact address and travel time, and allow extra time during rush hour or service disruptions.

Are there Chinese- and Korean-speaking communities in North York?

Yes. The Yonge Street corridor around Finch and Sheppard has many Chinese and Korean restaurants, shops and services, and many landlords and roommates speak Mandarin, Cantonese or Korean. That can make settling in easier for newcomers.
