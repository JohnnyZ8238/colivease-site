Etobicoke Rentals: Neighbourhoods, Transit and Rooms

Etobicoke rentals guide: Mimico and Humber Bay condos, rooms near Humber College, Line 2, Line 6 and GO commute options, plus what each neighbourhood is like.

Updated November 21, 2026 · 7 min read · ColivEase

Etobicoke rentals cover a lot of ground: glassy waterfront condos in Humber Bay Shores, character apartments and houses in Mimico and Long Branch, high-rises around Islington and Kipling stations, and rooms in shared houses near Humber Polytechnic's North campus. Etobicoke is the western part of the City of Toronto, so you keep TTC service and Toronto tenant protections while often getting more space and greenery than downtown. Getting around is easiest near Line 2, the new Line 6 Finch West or a GO station.

Key takeaways

  • South Etobicoke (Humber Bay Shores, Mimico, New Toronto, Long Branch) is the lakeside, condo-and-character-house side, with GO and streetcar service.
  • Central Etobicoke around Line 2 (Islington, Kipling) has apartment towers, condos and a direct subway ride downtown.
  • North Etobicoke (Rexdale, around Humber's North campus) is generally more affordable, and Line 6 Finch West now connects it to Line 1.
  • Humber Polytechnic has two Etobicoke campuses, North and Lakeshore, so student rooms are spread across both ends.
  • Older apartment towers of three or more storeys and 10 or more units fall under the City's RentSafeTO program, which posts building evaluation scores.

What Etobicoke rentals cost

Rents vary a lot by area and building age. As a reference point, liv.rent's May 2026 Ontario rent report put Etobicoke's average unfurnished one-bedroom at about $2,176 a month. A room in a shared house, especially in north Etobicoke, typically costs less; a new waterfront condo usually costs more. Toronto-wide asking rents have eased over the past year, so compare a few listings before you commit.

Mimico and Humber Bay condos

The lakeshore around Humber Bay Shores, near Park Lawn Road and Lake Shore Boulevard West, is one of Toronto's biggest condo clusters outside the core. Renters like the waterfront trail, Humber Bay Park and the skyline views. Just west, Mimico feels more like a village, with low-rise buildings, independent shops and Mimico GO station.

What to check when renting a condo here:

  • Condo rules: as a tenant you must follow them. Ask for a copy, including move-in and elevator booking rules.
  • What's included: heat, hydro, water, parking and storage locker. Many condos sub-meter electricity.
  • Owner or agent? Confirm the person renting to you owns the unit or is a licensed agent acting for the owner.
  • Sharing with roommates: check whether everyone will be on the lease, and agree on the split before signing.
  • Commute at rush hour: the Gardiner Expressway and Lake Shore Boulevard can get congested; test the trip at the time you'll actually travel.

Rooms near Humber College

Humber, now Humber Polytechnic, has two main campuses in Etobicoke:

North campus (Highway 27 and Finch Avenue West area)

The North campus, shared with the University of Guelph-Humber, is in north Etobicoke. Most off-campus renters share houses in the surrounding neighbourhoods, often renting a room with a shared kitchen. Line 6 Finch West opened on December 7, 2025, running about 11 km from Finch West station on Line 1 to Humber College station at the North campus, with stops along Finch Avenue West including near Etobicoke General Hospital. That makes rooms along Finch West much more practical for students without a car, and links the campus to Line 1 and York University's area.

Lakeshore campus (south Etobicoke)

The Lakeshore campus sits in south Etobicoke on Lake Shore Boulevard West near Kipling Avenue, in the New Toronto area, a short trip from Long Branch. Students here often rent rooms or basement apartments in nearby houses, or share apartments in older low-rise buildings. Long Branch and Mimico GO stations and the Lake Shore streetcar connect the area to downtown.

Tips for students renting near either campus:

  1. Check the exact walk or transit time, not just the distance; north Etobicoke blocks are long.
  2. Ask whether the landlord lives in the house. If you share a kitchen or bathroom with the owner or their family, Ontario's Residential Tenancies Act generally doesn't apply.
  3. Use your school's off-campus housing service as a starting point, and verify every landlord yourself.
  4. Never pay a deposit before viewing in person or by live video.

Older apartment towers: check RentSafeTO

Central and north Etobicoke have many older purpose-built rental towers, often with larger units than new condos. The City of Toronto's RentSafeTO program applies to apartment buildings with three or more storeys and 10 or more units. You can look up a building's evaluation score before you apply, and if repairs aren't handled, tenants can submit a written service request to the landlord and then contact 311.

Is Etobicoke right for you?

Choose Etobicoke if you want more space or a lakeside setting while staying inside Toronto, if you study at Humber, or if you split your week between downtown and Mississauga or the airport. Frequently asked questions

Is Etobicoke a good place to rent?

For many people, yes. Etobicoke offers waterfront condos, quieter residential streets, direct subway and GO connections to downtown and quick access to Pearson Airport and Mississauga. The trade-off is that northern and western parts are more spread out, so check transit for the exact address.

How much is rent in Etobicoke?

It depends on the area and unit type. liv.rent's May 2026 Ontario rent report put Etobicoke's average unfurnished one-bedroom at about $2,176 a month. Rooms in shared houses usually cost less, and new waterfront condos often cost more.

Does the subway go to Humber's North campus?

Not the subway itself, but Line 6 Finch West, a light rail line that opened on December 7, 2025, runs from Finch West station on Line 1 to Humber College station at Humber Polytechnic's North campus. Several TTC bus routes also serve the campus.

Is Humber Bay Shores good for commuting downtown?

It can be. Mimico GO station is a short trip west for a quick train ride to Union Station, and streetcar service runs along Lake Shore Boulevard West. Driving is convenient via the Gardiner Expressway, but rush-hour traffic can be heavy.
