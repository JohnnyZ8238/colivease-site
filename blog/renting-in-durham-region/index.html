Renting in Durham Region: Pickering, Ajax, Whitby, Oshawa

Rentals in Pickering, Ajax, Whitby and Oshawa: how each town compares, Oshawa rooms near Ontario Tech and Durham College, GO commute tips and rental licensing.

Updated November 21, 2026 · 7 min read · ColivEase

Looking for rentals in Pickering, Ajax, Whitby or Oshawa? Durham Region, just east of Toronto, offers more space for the money in many areas, a GO train line that links all four downtowns to Union Station, and a large student market around Ontario Tech University and Durham College in north Oshawa. Pickering and Ajax suit Toronto commuters best, while Oshawa is usually the most affordable and the natural choice for students.

Key takeaways

  • Pickering and Ajax are the closest to Toronto and the most commuter-friendly, but Pickering is also one of the priciest rental markets in Ontario outside the city.
  • Whitby balances commute time and space, with an established downtown and newer subdivisions to the north.
  • Oshawa is home to Ontario Tech University and Durham College, with lots of rooms in shared houses and generally lower rents.
  • The Lakeshore East GO line stops in all four towns. Live near a station and you can skip the car.
  • In Oshawa's campus area, rental properties need a City licence. Check before you sign.

What rentals cost in Pickering, Ajax, Whitby and Oshawa

Rents vary a lot by town and type of unit. A few reference points from recent reports:

  • Pickering: Rentals.ca's April 2026 report put Pickering's average asking rent at $2,591, the most expensive rental market in Ontario outside Toronto.
  • Oshawa: a September 2026 report from Rentals.ca and Urbanation found average Oshawa rents down about 10.8% from a year earlier, according to CP24's coverage.

These are averages of advertised rents across unit types, so a room in a shared house will cost much less, and a new condo more. Check current listings before you budget. Rents across the GTA have eased over the past two years, which can give renters a little more room to negotiate.

Pickering to Toronto commute by GO

The Lakeshore East GO line runs through Pickering, Ajax, Whitby and Oshawa to Union Station, with trains in both directions throughout the day. Pickering and Ajax have the shortest rides; Whitby and Oshawa add time. Exact travel times depend on whether you catch an express, so use GO's trip planner for the train you'd actually take.

Commuting tips:

  • Walk or bus to the station. GO station parking can fill up on weekday mornings, so a home within walking distance or on a direct bus route saves stress.
  • Count the fare. A GO commute costs more per trip than a TTC ride. Add it to your monthly budget when comparing with a Toronto rental.
  • Driving? Highway 401 is the main route west. Tolls were removed on the provincially owned Highway 407 East (from around Brock Road in Pickering east into Clarington) on June 1, 2025; the 407 ETR west of Brock Road is still tolled.
  • Local buses: Durham Region Transit (DRT) runs local routes and Pulse rapid bus service along Highway 2.

Renting near Ontario Tech University and Durham College

Ontario Tech University and Durham College share a campus in north Oshawa, around Simcoe Street North. Ontario Tech also has a downtown Oshawa location, and Durham College has a campus in Whitby. Trent University also has a Durham campus in Oshawa.

Oshawa rooms for rent

Most student renters in Oshawa share a house: each person rents a room and shares the kitchen and living space. Common areas to look:

  • Near the north campus, within walking distance or a short bus ride, including the Windfields area. Most convenient, and where most student houses are.
  • Along Simcoe Street between campus and downtown, with frequent buses.
  • Downtown Oshawa, close to the downtown university buildings, the Oshawa bus terminal and amenities, with older apartments and houses.

Check the rental licence

The City of Oshawa requires residential rental properties in the area around Durham College and Ontario Tech University to be licensed. The program sets minimum standards for health, safety and property maintenance, and the City publishes information about licensed properties. Before you sign, ask the landlord for the licence and compare it with the City's records. A licensed house has been inspected; an unlicensed one may have unknown safety issues.

Basement apartments

Many Durham houses have basement units. Before you rent one, check for a safe second exit, working smoke and carbon monoxide alarms and enough ceiling height. Our guide to legal basement apartments explains what to look for; the safety basics apply across Ontario.

Where to live in each town

Pickering: the City Centre around Pickering Town Centre and the GO station has the most condos and the easiest commute. Bay Ridges, south by the lake, is older and quieter. Seaton, in north Pickering, is a newer community best suited to drivers.

Ajax: neighbourhoods near the GO station and along the waterfront are popular with commuters. Pickering Village, a historic area of Ajax along Kingston Road, has a small-town main street feel.

Whitby: downtown Whitby, around Brock and Dundas streets, offers older houses, apartments and walkable shops. Brooklin, in north Whitby, is mostly newer family homes, many with basement units.

Oshawa: north Oshawa is the student hub; downtown Oshawa has many apartments and the best transit connections; Oshawa GO station is in the southwest, near Highway 401, so check your bus connection if you plan to commute to Toronto.

Renting tips for Durham

  • Use the Ontario Standard Lease. Most residential tenancies in Ontario need one, including rooms in houses where the landlord doesn't live.
  • Ask about utilities. In houses, rent may or may not include hydro, gas, water and internet.
  • Check winter commutes. A 15-minute walk to the GO station feels longer in January.
  • Verify before paying. View in person or by live video, and never send a deposit before signing.

Frequently asked questions

Is it cheaper to rent in Durham Region than in Toronto?

Often, but not everywhere. Rentals.ca's April 2026 report put Pickering's average asking rent at $2,591, the highest in Ontario outside Toronto, while Oshawa has generally been more affordable and saw rents fall over the past year. Compare total costs, including GO fares and parking, not just rent.

Do I need a car to live in Durham Region?

Not if you live near a GO station or a frequent bus route. Downtown Pickering, Ajax, Whitby and Oshawa all have GO train stations, and Durham Region Transit runs local and rapid bus service. Areas further north, such as Brooklin or Seaton, are much easier with a car.

How do I check if a student house in Oshawa is licensed?

The City of Oshawa requires a licence for residential rental properties in the area around Durham College and Ontario Tech University, and publishes information about licensed properties on its Residential Rental Housing page. Ask the landlord for the licence before you sign, and check it against the City's information.

Which Durham town is best for working in downtown Toronto?

Pickering and Ajax are closest, so they usually give the shortest GO train rides to Union Station. Whitby and Oshawa add travel time but tend to offer more space for the money. Pick a home within a walk or short bus ride of the station.
